QoE Prediction of Encrypted Video Traffic for Interactive and Impatient Streaming Users

ABSTRACT

Existing QoE predictors fail when users jump during playback - each jump flushes the buffer and breaks the stationary traffic assumptions prior models rely on. This work introduces PB-QoE, a jump-aware pipeline that detects interaction intensity from encrypted traffic with 100% accuracy, then corrects QoE estimates via an interaction-dependent penalty - outperforming baselines across all tested bandwidth and jump conditions.

01 — MOTIVATION

The gap prior models ignore

End-to-end encryption already makes QoE estimation hard. Add the reality that viewers jump, skip, and replay - and the problem becomes fundamentally different.

PRIOR ASSUMPTION

Smooth, linear playback

Existing models are trained and evaluated on uninterrupted sessions where the player fetches segments in order, buffers predictably, and adapts bitrate smoothly.

  • Steady buffer fill cycles
  • Predictable ABR adaptation
  • Stationary traffic patterns
  • High accuracy in controlled settings
REAL WORLD REALITY

Interactive, jumping users

Real viewers frequently skip forward or replay content - especially for longer-form video. Each jump invalidates buffered data and triggers bursty, non-stationary traffic.

  • Buffer flushes and aggressive refills
  • ABR reconfiguration after each jump
  • Non-stationary traffic patterns
  • Accuracy collapses to <20% at 4 jumps
02 — CONTRIBUTIONS

Four key findings

01

First systematic study of QoE prediction on encrypted VoD traffic that explicitly accounts for user jumping behaviour.

02

State-of-the-art QoE classifiers trained on smooth playback degrade sharply when jumps occur - revealing a clear generalisation gap.

03

Jump events and interaction intensity can be reliably inferred from encrypted traffic alone, across all bandwidth regimes tested.

04

Introduces PB-QoE - a jump-aware pipeline combining regression with an interaction-dependent penalty - achieving robustness under highly interactive playback.

03 — METHODOLOGY

The PB-QoE pipeline

Three components work together: a regression head captures continuous QoE, a jump estimator infers interaction severity, and a penalty term corrects for interaction-induced degradation.

RF Regression Head

STEP 03
↯

Jump-Count Estimator

Lightweight classifier predicts J ∈ {0, 1, 2, 4} - detecting interaction intensity from encrypted traffic alone.

OUTPUT
◉

PB-QoE Score

Jump-aware correction applied to regression output, yielding robust final QoE estimate.

QoEPB = QoEreg − α · J   where J = estimated jump count, α = per-jump penalty coefficient
