2025 IFIP Networking · Limassol, Cyprus

Predicting Video QoE from Encrypted Traffic: Leveraging Video Fingerprinting and Providing System-Level Insights

End-to-end encryption blocks network operators from accessing video quality metrics, forcing reliance on indirect traffic data for QoE estimation. This work adapts state-of-the-art video fingerprinting techniques to predict QoE from encrypted streams, achieving high accuracy across diverse and fluctuating network conditions. A systematic analysis confirms the approach remains robust with limited training data, making it practical for real-world deployment.

📄 Somiya Kapoor, David Hasselquist, Ethan Witwer, Mikael Asplund, Niklas Carlsson
Linköping University & Sectra Communications

2026 IFIP Networking 2026 · Lugano, Switzerland

QoE Prediction of Encrypted Video Traffic for Interactive and Impatient Streaming Users

Existing QoE predictors fail when users jump during playback - each jump flushes the buffer and breaks the stationary traffic assumptions prior models rely on. This work introduces PB-QoE, a jump-aware pipeline that detects interaction intensity from encrypted traffic with 100% accuracy, then corrects QoE estimates via an interaction-dependent penalty - outperforming baselines across all tested bandwidth and jump conditions.

📄 Somiya Kapoor, David Hasselquist, Ethan Witwer, Mikael Asplund, Niklas Carlsson
Linköping University & Sectra Communications
