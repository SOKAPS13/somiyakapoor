BIO

My name is Somiya Kapoor. I'm currently working as a PhD Student at Linköping University, Sweden.

Professionally, I'm focused on computer science and network security — drawn to understanding how networks behave and how that knowledge can be applied to real-world problems. My research is centred on traffic analysis, network fingerprinting, and Quality of Experience (QoE) prediction, exploring how network traffic patterns can reveal information and how user experience can be measured and anticipated.

Some of my personal interests include travelling and experiencing new places, playing badminton, and seeking out adventures wherever I can find them. The big theme, I would say, is a love for exploration — whether that's on a badminton court, on the road, or somewhere a little off the beaten path.
